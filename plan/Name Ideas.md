-   Hunter's Lodge
    -   The Hunt Lodge
    -   The Lodge
-   Hunter's Haven
    -   Haven
-   Investigate a Mystery (Only considered because it's the move that defines the game)
-   Monster of the Sheets (This is such a bad pun but I love it) (New working title)
-   The Corkboard
-   Seven Luck (Every hunter starts with 7 Luck, and losing it all is their doom. Not "sheet" but it's short, evocative, and clever)
-   +Weird (Punchy but probably to specific; Only considered because it's the rating that defines the game)

# Criteria

-   Should immediately evoke:
    -   Specifically a _character sheet_ app (not for session notes, case logs, lore, etc)
    -   for _Monster of the Week_ (should obviously be a reference to the game for anyone familiar with it)
    -   for hunters (players), not primarily keepers (GMs)
-   Should be applicable to all playbooks, not just a few
    -   This means avoid names that are just one stat or basic move, since those are more biased to some playbooks than others
    -   Note that the list above includes "Haven", which is specifically a move from the Expert playbook. This is a acceptable exception to this rule:
        -   The Haven is a move that's shared with the other hunters at the table, and functions as "home".
        -   Also, multiple playbooks can gain havens through improvements.
-   Ideally, short and punchy, 1-2 words, 2-3 syllables

## Rejected

-   Boring, uninspired, not punchy (e.g. just adding the word "hunter" to another word):
    -   Hunter's Playbook
    -   Pocket Playbook
    -   Hunter Dossier
    -   Hunter Folio
-   Failures at wordplay:
    -   Kick Some Sheet
    -   Hunter of the Week
